# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Imported deals become searchable (the automatic review of imports): an offer imported from a configured feed source is checked when it is imported and passes when its link is https and points at that source's retailer domain, its title is not empty and at most 300 characters, its price is a positive number with a currency, it has not expired and it states none of the blocked/adult terms. A pass sets `review_state = auto_approved` (published, indexable, `reviewed_by = system:import-checks`); a fail stays `pending` with the failed check's name in `review_failed_check`. Offers people submit by hand keep today's review flow and a moderator can still hide any offer. `server/domain/import-review.js`, migration `0009_import_auto_review.sql`, `scripts/backfill-import-review.js` (batches of 500, `--dry-run`, counts only), `test/import-review.test.js`.

- `llms.txt` lists only URLs that answer (SEO-34): the *Product JSON* link pointed at `/sitemaps/products.xml`, which no longer exists once the sitemap is an index, and `/sitemap.xml` itself was a single 24,908-URL file a crawler waited out. The link now names the index, and `test/pages.test.js` sweeps every URL in the file for an answer below 400.
- The sitemaps are a bounded index (SEO-16): `GET /sitemap.xml` (openvibe-shared `seo.sitemapIndexXml`) names `/sitemap-pages.xml` and one `/sitemap-<n>.xml` child per 10,000 catalog URLs — every indexable offer, then every product, in the order the site has always listed them — so no child can pass a crawler's per-file limit and the union of the children is exactly the old catalog sitemap. A chunk number past the last answers 404; `robots.txt` still names `/sitemap.xml` and the content type and `cache-policy` headers are unchanged. `test/sitemap-build.test.js` seeds 20,005 offers and a product and pins all of it.
- The front page is curated (`server/render/home.js`): a search and the site's numbers beside the hero, then editors' picks, hot, just in, ending soon, under $20, categories, shops and crowdfunding, how it works and six questions (FAQPage and ItemList JSON-LD). The hot list moved to `/hot` (`/?page=N` → 301); new `/ending`, `/picks`, `/c` and `/c/:category`; `/sitemaps/pages.xml` lists them all (the sitemap had no page a person browses). Imported deals keep their source's category and editors' pick (Sources feed@3; migration `0007_offer_category_pick.sql` adds `staff_pick`). Budgets: html 29.1 KB, css 24.2 KB.
- Account export and deletion (ADR-033): `network.account.export_requested` and `network.account.deleted` are answered at `POST /internal/events` by `server/domain/account-data.js` over `openvibe-sdk/account-data` (SDK v0.37.0, was v0.35.0). The person's watches, votes, name and rate windows are deleted; what they contributed to the shared catalog stays without their id; staff moderation records stay attributed. The two subscriptions are created at boot. Migration `0003_account_data.sql` adds `account_data_events`. `test/account-data.test.js`.
- openvibe-contracts moves from v0.79.0 to v0.97.0 (pin, lockfile and `node_modules`); nothing in the range breaks Deals, and the contracts' own service check is green. All twelve `deals.*` ids are now defined by the release, so `server/auth/capabilities.js` drops its local fallback for proposed ids and sends every check through the library's grant rule; `test/capabilities.test.js` pins that every guarded id is defined and that exact, prefix, denied and unknown ids answer as `capabilities.check()` does. README and STATUS.json name v0.97.0.
- Every page is rendered through `openvibe-publishing/layout` (v1.2.0, on `openvibe-shared/shell` v2.6.0): the head, the Frame, the noscript navigation, the footer and its init come from the shared document; robots and the canonical still come from the indexability gate's decision. Styling is unchanged.
